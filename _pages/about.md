# Biography

I am a researcher specializing in **Bacteriophage Biology and Bioinformatics**, with a focus on studying bacteriophage diversity through metaviromics & culturomics.

Currently, this site serves as an interactive **Bacteriophage Atlas** documenting my collection of 44 bacteriophages isolated against ESKAPE pathogens and their diverse virion structural morphology as revealed through Transmission Electron Microscopy (TEM).

* **PhD:** Bacteriology & Bioinformatics
* **Expertise:** Bacteriophage Isolation & TEM, Bacteriophage Genomics, Viral Metagenomics, & Bacterial Multi-omics
